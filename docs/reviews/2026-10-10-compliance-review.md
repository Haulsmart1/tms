# Compliance source review — 2026-10-10

Source review only; no live records, qualifications, carrier bookings or regulatory approval were changed. Sources are primary government/regulator publications. The registry in `lib/compliance/sources.ts` distinguishes regulatory rule, operator policy, carrier policy, guidance and unverified information. Null effective dates mean a single legal commencement date has not been certified, not that a rule is timeless.

The [DVSA assimilated/AETR manual](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/drivers-hours-goods-vehicles/1-assimilated-and-aetr-rules-on-drivers-hours), updated July 2026, extends scope to certain UK–EU international light-goods operations above 2.5 tonnes from 1 July 2026, with exemptions. The existing classifier recognises the weight/scope baseline but lacks sufficient own-account/activity/exemption and effective journey-date facts for universal certification. This is an explicit review gap; its newer scheduling semantics were not overwritten. The [tachograph chapter](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/drivers-hours-goods-vehicles/4-tachograph-rules) supplies equipment requirements; fitted/not-fitted metadata alone cannot prove equipment eligibility.

[DfT working-time guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/working-time-regulations-for-mobile-workers/working-time-rules-lorry-bus-and-coach-drivers-and-crew) distinguishes working time from driving time, including reference-period and agreement conditions. Those conditions should not be inferred from a single planning lane.

[Government dangerous-goods classes](https://www.gov.uk/moving-dangerous-goods/what-are-dangerous-goods) identifies Class 1 explosives and Class 7 radioactive material. [ONR transport legislation](https://www.onr.org.uk/our-work/what-we-regulate/transport-of-radioactive-material/transport-legislation) explains the civil radioactive-transport authority. Neither source makes a customer ADR flag a movement approval. UNECE ADR text retrieval was blocked; chapter-specific ADR thresholds remain UNVERIFIED in this review.
